Accessories and Connectivity

The right accessories can improve productivity, protect your device and make everyday tasks easier. Choose accessories according to your laptop's available ports, operating system and actual needs.

Useful Accessories

Accessory How It Helps What to Check
Laptop bag or sleeve Protects the laptop while travelling between home, campus and work. Correct screen size, padding, water resistance and build quality.
Wireless mouse Can make navigation, design work and long sessions more comfortable. Bluetooth or USB receiver compatibility and battery life.
External keyboard Provides a separate typing position for a desk-based setup. Connection type, layout and available desk space.
USB hub or dock Adds connection options for displays, storage and other peripherals. USB-C/USB-A support, display output and power-delivery limits.
Headset or webcam Supports online classes, meetings, calls and collaboration. Microphone clarity, comfort and connection compatibility.
External storage Provides extra space and a way to keep separate file backups. Capacity, transfer speed, encryption and backup habits.

A Simple Desk Setup

For a study or office desk, a laptop stand, external keyboard, mouse and headset can create a more comfortable workspace. A USB hub may help connect several devices, but check its supported ports and power limits before buying.

Compatibility reminder: Not every accessory works with every laptop. Confirm the port type, supported display output, charging requirements and operating-system compatibility. Avoid forcing connectors or using untrusted chargers.

Accessory examples are general guidance, not included items or confirmed TechNova bundle offers.
