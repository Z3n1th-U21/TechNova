TechNova AirBook

Light. Powerful. Stylish. A practical companion for everyday productivity.

Price: ₹59,990

The AirBook is designed for users who need a reliable laptop for classes, assignments, browsing, video meetings and office applications. Its compact screen size and solid-state storage make it suitable for carrying between home, campus and work.

Key Specifications

  • 13.3-inch Full HD display for clear text and comfortable viewing.
  • Intel Core i5-class processor for everyday multitasking.
  • Up to 16GB RAM, depending on configuration.
  • 512GB solid-state drive for responsive booting and file access.
  • Slim, portable design for travel and daily use.

Ideal User

Students, teachers, office professionals and users who value portability for documents, spreadsheets, presentations, research and online meetings.

Product highlight: A balanced everyday laptop should make routine work feel simple: open documents quickly, keep several browser tabs available and move easily from one task to another.
