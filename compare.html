Quick Buying Checklist

  • Processor: choose according to the applications you use.
  • RAM: more memory helps when many apps and browser tabs are open.
  • Storage: SSDs generally provide faster everyday responsiveness than traditional hard drives.
  • Display: consider size, resolution, brightness and refresh rate.
  • Battery and weight: important if you carry the laptop frequently.
  • Ports and connectivity: check USB, HDMI, audio and wireless requirements.

Tip: Do not choose only by processor name. Compare the complete configuration and the work you expect the laptop to perform.
