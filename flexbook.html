TechNova FlexBook

Work. Create. Share. A flexible laptop for changing ways of working.

Price: ₹69,990

FlexBook is a convertible 2-in-1 concept that combines a laptop-style keyboard with touchscreen interaction. It is intended for users who take notes, read digital material, sketch ideas, present slides or collaborate in different settings.

Key Specifications

  • 14-inch touchscreen display for direct interaction.
  • Intel Core i5-class processor for productivity tasks.
  • 8GB RAM for everyday multitasking.
  • 512GB SSD for documents, apps and personal files.
  • Convertible hinge design for laptop and presentation modes.

Ideal User

Students, presenters, teachers and mobile professionals who benefit from touch input and flexible viewing positions.

Product highlight: The 2-in-1 format can make it convenient to switch from typing an assignment to reading notes or showing a presentation to a small group.
