A Simple Laptop Buying Guide

  1. Identify your main use
    List the tasks you perform most often: online classes, office work, coding, gaming, editing or presentations.
  2. Set a realistic budget
    Decide how much you can spend, including accessories and any software you may need.
  3. Compare configurations
    Check the exact processor, RAM, storage, graphics and display rather than relying only on the model name.
  4. Check comfort and build
    If possible, try the keyboard, trackpad, screen and hinge. Consider the laptop's weight.
  5. Review service and warranty
    Understand the warranty period, service process, accidental-damage coverage and return policy.
  6. Compare the final price
    Check whether the price includes taxes, delivery, accessories or promotional conditions.

Smart shopping reminder: A laptop is a long-term purchase. A slightly more suitable configuration can be better value than a cheaper device that does not meet your needs.
