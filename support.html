Care, Safety and Support

Everyday Care

  • Carry the laptop in a padded bag and avoid placing heavy objects on it.
  • Keep liquids, food crumbs and dust away from the keyboard and ports.
  • Use the laptop on a firm surface so ventilation openings are not blocked.
  • Shut down or restart periodically and install trusted software updates.
  • Use a surge protector where appropriate and handle the charger carefully.
  • Back up important files to a secure external drive or trusted cloud service.

Digital Safety

Use unique passwords, enable multi-factor authentication when available, avoid suspicious links and downloads, and lock the screen when stepping away. Download applications from trusted sources and keep security protection up to date.

Support Promise
