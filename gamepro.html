TechNova GamePro

Play beyond limits. Built for users who want more graphics and speed.

Price: ₹89,990

GamePro is the performance-focused model in the TechNova range. It is aimed at gamers and users working with graphics-heavy applications. A high-refresh-rate display can make supported games appear smoother, while dedicated graphics hardware can help with visual workloads.

Key Specifications

  • 15.6-inch Full HD display with an illustrative 144Hz refresh rate.
  • Intel Core i7-class processor.
  • 16GB RAM for multitasking and demanding applications.
  • 1TB SSD for games, projects and media files.
  • Dedicated RTX-class graphics (exact GPU model depends on configuration).

Ideal User

Gamers, streamers, engineering students and creators who use supported 3D, editing or graphics software.

Before buying: Check the exact graphics card, cooling design, power adapter, display coverage and warranty terms. Gaming laptops can be heavier and may have shorter battery life under heavy load.
